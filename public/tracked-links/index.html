In the tool

Build a link in five steps

Open the UTM Generator and stay on the Build a link tab.
1

Add where the link goes

For a tp.com page, pick tp.com page and type only the part after tp.com/, like en-us/industries/healthcare/. For a Marketo or other page, pick Landing page / unique URL and paste the full address. If the address has a ? in it, delete the ? and everything after it.

2

Pick where it's going, then how

Choose the source (LinkedIn, Demandbase, Marketo, event…), then the medium. The medium list only shows what fits the source, so you can't pick a wrong pair. Not sure? See Pick your situation.

3

Pick what you're promoting

Choose the content type, like white paper, case study, webinar or report.

4

Name the campaign

Set Market to us. Add a Partner only if a partner is part of the placement. Type your short name in a few plain words. Skip the market and the year; the tool adds both.

5

Pick the goal and save

Choose the objective (awareness, lead gen, nurture…) and click Generate & Save. Copy the long link or the short link, or download a QR code. It's saved to the Link log automatically.

Quick check before you share: everything after the ? should start with utm_. If you see gclid, gbraid, _gl or fbclid, rebuild from the clean page address.

One campaign, several channels

Bulk create

Running the same campaign on LinkedIn, Demandbase and email at once? Use the Bulk create tab. Enter the page, content type and campaign name once, tick each channel, and pick its medium and goal. Click Save selected. Every link lands in the Link log under the same campaign name, so the channels compare side by side. Download the CSV to hand the links to whoever is placing them.

Keep the numbers clean

Four don'ts

1

Don't type or edit links by hand

"LinkedIn", "linkedin" and "Linked In" show up as three different campaigns. The tool keeps the spelling consistent.

2

Don't copy an address straight from your browser bar

It often carries leftovers from an ad you clicked, which scrambles who gets credit. Start from the clean page address.

3

Don't put tracked links on tp.com pages

They're for links outside tp.com only. On our own pages they overwrite how the visitor really arrived.

4

Don't add the market or year to the short name

The tool adds both. Typing them gives names like us_usevent2026_202611.

Help

Questions

The tool won't take my short name.

Keep it to 30 characters and plain words. A few words, like "cross-network" or anything starting with "shop", are blocked because GA4 files them under the wrong channel.

My source, medium or content type isn't in the list.

The lists are locked so every market builds links the same way. Reach out to NA Performance Marketing and we'll get it reviewed.
