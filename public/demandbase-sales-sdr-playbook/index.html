The handoff note (paste into Salesforce)

  • Account, contact and title: who you spoke with.
  • Intent topic: what the account is researching.
  • What they said: pain, timing, current provider.
  • Next step: meeting date and attendees.
  • BD: the account owner taking it from here.
Prompts

AI prompts for SDRs

Where AI Chat is turned on for your group, copy a prompt, swap in your details, and go. Start with your full name and "US accounts only," because AI Chat doesn't know who "me" is and the platform is shared with other regions.

Every morning: your call list
I'm {your full name}, an SDR. From {list name}, US accounts only, show the accounts I should work first today with no sales touches in 14 days, ranked by engagement and Pipeline Predict, with the top topic of interest and the most engaged US contact for each.

Good looks like: a ranked call list with a topic and a name on every row. 15 to 25 rows is a day.

Before every dial
For {company}, give me a 60-second research brief: why it's surfacing (journey stage, engagement, intent topics), the high-value pages they visited, and any recent company news I can use as a hook.

Good looks like: one hook to open with. Company news beats a web visit as an opener.

Who to call first
For {company}, who should I reach out to first? Show the most engaged US-based contacts by persona, with titles and what they engaged with, and rank the top 3 with a reason for each.

Good looks like: three names with titles. Start with the VP or Director, not the C-suite.

Writing the outreach
Help me write a 4-part outreach email to {contact}, {title} at {company}. Their company is researching {topics}. Observation, problem, solution, ask, 1 to 2 sentences each, no section labels, non-salesy, under 120 words.

Good looks like: one ask. Edit the first line so it sounds like you, and never say we saw their web activity.

Who came back
I'm {your full name}. Show the US accounts I've sequenced that re-engaged in the last 14 days after going quiet, what they re-engaged with and who. Recommend a timely follow-up.

Good looks like: a short re-engagement list. These convert faster than cold, so call them first.

Where you're single-threaded
At the US accounts I'm working, where am I single-threaded? Show the buying-group personas I should add and a one-line angle for each.

Good looks like: account, missing persona, angle. Add one new persona per account per week.

The handoff
Build a handoff brief for {company} for my Business Developer: the engaged buying group, intent topics, what's been said so far, and the recommended next step. Keep it to one page.

Good looks like: one page the BD reads in a minute. Paste it into the meeting invite.

Rules of the road

Rules of the road

  • Log every touch in Salesforce. The no-sales-touch filter only counts activities logged in Salesforce or synced through Demandbase email and calendar logging.
  • Check five rows against the grid or the account page before you build a call list from an answer.
  • Credits: every prompt spends AI credits, and every follow-up counts as a new prompt. One well-built prompt beats five quick ones. The priority actions queue and the account and person panels are free.
  • Never tell a prospect we saw their web activity.
  • Something broken? Send a screenshot to NA Performance Marketing the day you see it.
