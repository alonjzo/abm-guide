Who's the parent?

Physical event or trade show

Always the parent. Emails, ads, landing pages and SDR follow-up go under it.

Webinar for an event

It's a child of that event, not a parent of its own.

Ad program that leads

The ad program is the parent. Its emails and SDR follow-up are children.

CEO-led roundtables

They sit under Strategy · Thought Leadership.

Five rules

  1. Nobody creates a new Strategy. Pick one from the list. Missing? Ask NA Performance Marketing.
  2. Name, type and place must agree. A campaign named Strategy is typed Strategy. A webinar or an ad carries its own type.
  3. One campaign per activity. An email sent for SDRs is two campaigns: the email, and the SDR follow-up.
  4. Don't create what you won't use. Empty campaigns show up as zeros in every report. Tests and cancelled work get marked Aborted.
  5. Set the status after every event. Who registered, who attended, who met. No status, no credit.
