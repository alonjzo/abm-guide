Why this works

Spend per account predicts almost everything

Across 9,849 accounts reached in FY26, how much we spent per account tracked lift almost perfectly — from 7.2% at the bottom to 59.0% at the top. That is an 8x spread, and it is the most reliable relationship in our advertising data.

Read the trade-off honestly. Concentrating raises the lift rate but shrinks the base. Going deeper does not automatically move more accounts — it moves a higher share of fewer accounts. The calculator shows both numbers so you can pick deliberately instead of by accident.

What this does not tell you. Lift is an engagement signal, not pipeline. A moved account is one showing more activity after the campaign than before — it is not a lead, an opportunity or revenue. And a moved Tier 2 account is worth more than a moved Tier 5, which a headcount cannot capture.
