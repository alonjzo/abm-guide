What each tab is for

MQL, SQL and SAL each have their own dashboard tab. Their guidance is grouped together here.

Understand the terms

Glossary

Use this tab to get familiar with MQL, SQL, SAL, Net New and the dashboard’s conversion measures.

Before comparing two numbers, make sure they mean the same thing.

Read the stage definitions

Understand where qualification ends and sales acceptance begins.

Check the measure

Know whether you are looking at people, opportunities, wins or revenue.

Keep the comparison fair

Use the same market, period and measure when comparing results.

See the headline results

Choose your question

Take the date with you

Find your market

Sub-Region Overview

See funnel activity, opportunities, wins and financial measures for each region and sub-region.

For the US sub-region, read CTSS → US. The CTSS subtotal covers a wider group.

Find the right row

Keep US, Canada and the other named sub-regions separate.

Read across the row

Compare your market’s funnel and opportunity results in one place.

Keep revenue labels attached

Projected revenue, pipeline and annualized amounts are different measures. Use the column label and currency shown.

Understand a lead-stage result

MQL / SQL / SAL

These are three separate dashboard tabs. Each helps you see the stage breakdown by region and marketing source.

Choose the same region and sub-region before comparing the three tabs.

Select the stage

Open the tab for the part of the funnel you want to understand.

Narrow the view

Use the region and sub-region filters to focus on your market.

Look at the detail

Use the available record detail or Load more widgets to investigate an example.

See reported campaign engagement

Nurtured by Campaign

Explore campaign activity across leads and contacts, with regional and marketing-source breakdowns.

A missing result needs investigation; it does not automatically mean no work happened.

Find your activity

Look for the relevant campaign or marketing category.

Check your filters

Confirm you are viewing the intended market and period.

Send a useful example

If something looks missing, include the campaign link and an example in the request form.

Follow marketing into opportunities

Opportunity

Explore marketing-related opportunities, their classifications, and the won results reported on the dashboard.

Keep opportunity counts, won deals and financial amounts separate when presenting results.

Read the classification

Distinguish marketing-generated and nurtured activity using the dashboard definitions.

Use the displayed categories

Read Won and the other categories as labelled. Do not combine them into a single win number.

Name the financial measure

Keep the displayed revenue label and currency with the amount you share.

Review a particular campaign

Campaign Analysis

Filter by campaign name, type, target market, LOB, industry and dates to find the view you need.

This view starts from January 2024. Its totals cover a different period from the headline view.

Choose the campaign

Start with the exact name or the group of campaigns you need.

Set the scope

Apply the market and date filters before reading the totals.

Keep the context

Share the campaign link and selected filters with your findings.

Trace a campaign into a deal

Campaign Opps by BD/CS

Use this view to see campaign-related opportunities, their owners, stages, amounts and dates.

This is an all-time view unless you narrow it with the available filters.

Find the opportunity

Use the campaign and opportunity details to locate the deal.

Review the outcome

Read the owner, stage and financial measure together.

Use the right period

Apply the relevant dates for your question before comparing with another view.
