The part that stops builds

Only a quarter of our accounts have people

8,213 of 10,972 accounts carry zero known contacts. You can target them with account-based advertising. You cannot build a person audience, you cannot push contacts to LinkedIn, and you cannot hand a rep a name.

Run account-based targeting there. The person layer is yours to build — if you are unsure how, Demandbase Operations will walk you through it, but the list stays your work because you are the one who has to read the result.

Nearly half the list is CEO or President. For CX outsourcing and contact-center transformation that is rarely the entry point — the buying group is CX Leaders, LOB Leaders, Vertical Specialists, plus Procurement and Finance.

Two readings and the data cannot separate them: either the list was built with a seniority filter that over-weighted C-level, or mid-market genuinely lists a lot of Presidents and Owners. T2 and T3 are 74% of the people, which points at the second. Worth confirming before anyone maps these to buying groups — it is the same failure that let LinkedIn’s Owner and Founder buckets pull in independent consultants.

The file itself is clean. 9,946 people, 100% with email, 99.5% with title, 100% with domain. This is not a data-quality problem. It is a coverage problem — 75% of accounts simply are not in it.
