Interacted is not a subset of Visited. It is measured against a rolling 30-day baseline, not against the funnel above it. An account can be Interacted without appearing in Visited in the same window. If you present the funnel as a strict descent, someone will find the row that breaks it — say this first.

Reached at 71% is the headline problem, not lift at 20%. Nearly a third of the accounts you paid to target never saw an impression. That is fixed before launch — upload five or more display sizes and widen job level — not by changing creative mid-flight.

Say it before someone asks

Known limits

Four constraints that are structural, not defects. Stating them up front is the difference between a readout that holds and one that gets picked apart.

Ad clicks are anonymous. Permanently.

Under GDPR there is no person-level identity behind a click. The only way to name a human is a form fill. If someone asks "who clicked," the answer is that the question cannot be answered from advertising data — not that we have not pulled it yet.

Affects — every click metric

Contact lists cannot be added to a running campaign.

The person audience is fixed at launch. If coverage was thin when you built it, it stays thin for the flight. This is a build-step decision, not a mid-flight lever.

Affects — reach, person targeting

Intent volume is over the platform cap.

Tenant keyword volume is running over the 2M/week ceiling, so some accounts are missing intent signal. That suppresses dynamic list membership and can dampen ad performance through no fault of the campaign. Kate has it escalated to engineering.

Affects — dynamic lists, lift

Clicks and visits will never reconcile.

Cookie consent rates and direct traffic break the line between them. This is expected behaviour. Do not report the gap as a discrepancy and do not let anyone else frame it as one.

Affects — any click-to-visit ratio

The one claim to never make: that advertising lift caused pipeline. Lift is an engagement signal measured against a 30-day baseline. It is directional, it is useful, and it is not attribution. Pipeline claims go through Campaign Influence and opportunity contact roles — a different system with a different basis.
