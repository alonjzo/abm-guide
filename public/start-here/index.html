Questions people actually ask

FAQ

If you were about to send a message asking one of these, the answer is here.

Is this for clients?
No. Internal enablement only. It carries our account counts, our buying group logic, and our naming problems. None of that is client-facing.
Why does the same service show up under three different names?
Because we sell the same capability at different altitudes — as a product you demo, a service you contract, and an advisory engagement. That is a deliberate commercial choice, not duplication. The Services A–Z tab lists every collision.
Can I build a LinkedIn audience myself?
Building the audience in Demandbase is admin-gated — ask Josh or Sharath. You point your ad set at it once it exists. And always push from Demandbase; never build the audience natively in LinkedIn.
Why did my campaign get clicks from people who make no sense?
Almost always a wide seniority filter. LinkedIn's Owner, Founder and Partner buckets pull in independent consultants who are in none of our buying groups. Confine the ad set to the pushed matched list and let the buying groups target. Full write-up in Best Practices.
Why are my conversions showing zero?
Known tracking gap, not an audience failure. The LinkedIn Insight Tag is missing from the 2026 Marketo landing pages but firing on the thank-you page. Impressions, clicks and CTR are accurate — conversions are not. Flag it before you launch.
What is our actual account universe?
10,155 across T2 to T5, or 10,855 including enterprise. The 11,600 figure sometimes quoted includes Tier 6, a parking lot of 776 accounts.
What is TaaS, exactly?
Technology Services. We build and run the estate the rest of it depends on — CCaaS, CaaS, Security-as-a-Service on Genesys, ServiceNow, Microsoft, Sprinklr. It is not advisory, and it is not a TP.ai product.
How current is any of this?
Service taxonomy mirrors tp.com/services. Account and contact counts were pulled live from Salesforce on 11 September 2026. Platform mechanics are verified against Demandbase and LinkedIn documentation. Anything time-sensitive carries its own source line.
Something here is wrong. Who do I tell?
Josh. Corrections are wanted — the point is that this becomes the place people trust instead of asking around.
