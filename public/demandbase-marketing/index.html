For each person return: full name, job title, email, contact information, company, tier, buying group, whether they're a decision maker, in-market status, journey stage, pipeline, and the assigned BD owner. Five rules that make prompts land

Get these right and the output is clean

Skip them and you get noise.

Spell out every acronym.

"Contact Center as a Service," not CCaaS; "Technology as a Service," not TAS.

Talk to it like a person.

Full sentences, real intent — not a pile of keywords.

Always bound it.

Name the timeframe and the geography (US + Canada) or you'll pull the world.

Ask for the fields you need.

Spell out the columns every time so lists come back BD-ready.

Start small, keep it weekly.

Run 2–3 plays you can manage. Prove them, then add.
