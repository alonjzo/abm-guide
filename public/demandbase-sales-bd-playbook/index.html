Your Monday

Your Monday in four clicks

  1. List picker: set it to your territory list.
  2. I sell: pick the buying group for your line of business. The people list reshapes to that committee.
  3. No sales touches (14d): turn it on, so two people don't call the same person in the same week.
  4. Preset: "Act on people that are highly engaged," then sort by engagement points.

How to read the grid

  • Marketing engagement points: 60 is hot, 40 is warm. Sort by this column.
  • Top activity: the one thing that scored them. That's your opening line.
  • Buying group / role: where they sit on the committee for the group you picked. Blank means you haven't picked one.
  • The read: high points plus a tp.com visit plus a buying-group role is a call today. High points from a campaign response means they opened our email, so it's softer.
The "top priority actions" banner is the SDR queue and isn't filtered to your own accounts. Work from your list, your saved preset, or AI Chat with your name in the prompt.
Builder

Build your own preset, click by click

  1. Click Builder at the top of the preset panel and choose People (a call sheet) or Accounts (a territory view).
  2. Make country is United States the first condition. The platform is shared with other regions.
  3. Add your accounts (owner is you, or your list), then the signal (engagement points over 40, or intent High on your line-of-business keyword set), then exclusions (no open opportunity, not a client).
  4. Watch the count. Under 50 is a call list. Over 300 means add a condition. Zero means a condition is wrong, usually a keyword set name.
  5. Name it so you can find it in a meeting, for example "AB Healthcare Hot People Nov," and save. It's one click every morning after that.
AI Chat

Three rules that make the answers right

1
Say who you areAI Chat doesn't know who "me" is. Start every prompt with your full name.
2
Say whereAdd "US accounts and North America contacts only." Without it you'll see people from LATAM and Asia Pacific.
3
Stay in one threadFollow-ups build on what it already knows. Your threads are saved on the left for 28 days.
Prompts

Your week in prompts

Copy, paste into AI Chat, fill in the braces. Run them in order through the week.

Monday: frame the week
I'm {your full name}, a Business Developer. From my US accounts only, identify my priority accounts most likely to convert to an opportunity. Rank by marketing engagement points (last 30 days) and Pipeline Predict score, and show journey stage, engagement points and top intent topic for each.

Good looks like: a ranked table, 10 to 30 rows, four columns of signal.

Monday: fresh and warm
I'm {your full name}. From my US accounts only, show accounts at MQA or earlier with the highest Pipeline Predict score and recent engagement. Flag any with fewer than 2 sales touches. Those are my fresh accounts this week.

Good looks like: a short list with a fresh flag. The flagged rows are your week.

Before every call
For {company}, what topics and services is the account most engaged with, why is it surfacing (journey stage, engagement trend, intent topics, high-value pages), and what are the 3 things I should know before I reach out? US contacts only.

Good looks like: three things you can say out loud. If it can't name a topic, the account is quiet.

Check the intent behind it
For {company}, are the account or its decision makers showing intent around {CX, customer service, contact center}? Show the topics and the dates.

Good looks like: topics with dates. Trending means it's happening now.

Who to call
At {company}, find the most engaged US-based people and the most engaged persona, then flag the buying-group personas where we have no engagement. Rank the top 3 people to reach, with a reason for each.

Good looks like: three names or roles with a reason each, plus the gap an SDR can fill.

First touch
Research {contact}'s priorities from recent company news, then draft a short email tying those priorities to the topics their company is researching. Observation, problem, solution, ask, 1 to 2 sentences each, no section labels, non-salesy, under 120 words.

Good looks like: four short parts and one ask. Edit it so it sounds like you, and never say we saw their web activity.

Midweek: your open deals
I'm {your full name}. Show my open opportunities on US accounts in Pipeline or SQL stage. For each: days in stage, Pipeline Predict trend, buying-group contacts engaged in the last 14 days, and any competitive intent. Flag the ones losing engagement and recommend the next best action.

Good looks like: your deals with a flag column. Competitive intent on an open deal is a call today.

Monthly: grow your clients
I'm {your full name}. For my closed-won US accounts, show the whitespace: services they're researching but don't buy from TP yet, and the contacts already engaged on them.

Good looks like: account, service gap, engaged contact. Ready for the account plan.

Friday: decide next week
I'm {your full name}. Give me my week: priority untouched US accounts, open opportunities that need attention, and the single most important next action for each.

Good looks like: one action per account. If it gives you more than one, ask it to pick.

Signals

What the signals mean

SignalWhat it tells you
Pipeline PredictHow likely the account is to open an opportunity soon. A high score means it's in market.
Trending intentResearch on a topic that's rising right now, with the dates it happened.
Journey stageWhere the account sits in the funnel, from aware through engaged, MQA and opportunity.
Engagement pointsHow much the account's people have engaged with TP: site visits, campaign responses, events.
AI account summaryOn any account page. Top intent at a glance. Copy it into AI Chat to go deeper.
Rules of the road

Rules of the road

  • Credits: every prompt spends AI credits from the pilot pool, and every follow-up counts as a new prompt. Send one well-built prompt, not five quick ones. Viewing account pages and summaries is free.
  • Check five rows against the account page before you build a call list from an answer.
  • Never tell a prospect we saw their web activity. Lead with their priorities and the topic.
  • Log every touch in Salesforce. The no-sales-touch filter only counts what's logged.
  • Something broken? Send a screenshot to NA Performance Marketing the day you see it.
