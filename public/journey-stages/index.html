Team Guide

US Journey stages — how they work
and what to do with them

Every account in the US Workspace sits in exactly one journey stage. This is what each stage means, how an account moves, and how it relates to what you build in campaigns.

i
Journey membership updates after a nightly evaluation, so changes to stage definitions show up the next day — which is why we make them over a weekend. Selector preview counts can also differ from final stage counts, because an account may qualify for a later stage.
01

The eight stages, in order

an account sits in exactly one
1
All Other
Doesn't match any other stage. The default, unqualified bucket.
2
Target Account
On a 2026 US Market target tier (T1-A or Tier 1–5). In our universe, nothing more.
3
Aware
Showing Demandbase keyword intent (High, Medium or Low) with activity in the last 90 days, on a US Market target tier. Research signal — not engagement with us.
4
Engaged Key
On a US Market target tier with ≥20 cumulative engagement points in the past 3 months, excluding Demandbase Keyword Intent, Geo Keyword Intent and Trending Intent. No known-person minimum.
5
MQA Key
Same tier requirement, ≥40 cumulative engagement points in the past 3 months and ≥1 Engaged Known Person. Same three intent exclusions. Intent alone does not qualify an account.
6
SQA Retired
Not in use. Retired intentionally — no accounts route here.
7
Opportunity
Has an open, active opportunity in the pipeline — a live deal the team is working.
8
Customer
An account we've won. Active customer.
02

One account, one stage — the highest it qualifies for

!
This is the thing people get wrong An account does not appear in two stages. It lands in the highest stage it qualifies for. So a Customer that is engaging heavily shows as Customer — not MQA, even though it clears every MQA condition. Same for an account with an open Opportunity.

If you are looking for an account in MQA and can't find it, check whether it's already further up the ladder. That is almost always the answer.
03

Campaigns and the journey are two different systems

this is the most common confusion
What you set in a campaign
Decides who sees your ads
→
Intent keywords — required field. Aims delivery at accounts researching those topics.
→
Contacts you already know — tells Demandbase to bid harder on named people.
→
Account lists, buying groups, job levels — all targeting inputs.
What the journey reads
Decides who qualifies
→
Engagement points from recorded activity — visits, clicks, opens, form fills, event attendance.
→
Intent is excluded from the point math on both Engaged and MQA. All three types.
→
Engaged Known People — people with point-bearing activity in the last 90 days.
Your campaign can move an account up the journey —
just not through intent. Through the recorded behavior it drives.
i
Adding someone to "Contacts you already know" does not make them an Engaged Known Person. That field is a bidding instruction. The person becomes "engaged" only when they do something that carries engagement points.

Three places show a count. They mean different things.

don't reconcile them
Where you see itWhat it actually counts
Selector badge
Journey Builder, next to Queue		 Accounts that meet this stage's rules — before precedence is applied. The "how many qualify" number. Recalculated daily.
Dashboard stage tile
ABX Dashboard headline		 Accounts that entered the stage during the selected date range. A flow metric, not a headcount. Forward transitions only, counted once.
Salesforce
Demandbase Journey Stage field		 A trailing mirror, up to ~24h behind — and it is fed by the Global journey, not the US Journey. Do not use it for US reporting.
!
A stage count and an "entered this quarter" count will never match, and that's correct — they answer different questions. If you need "how many accounts are in this stage right now," use the Accounts tab.
07

What changed in October 2026

StageWhat was wrongWhat it does now
Engaged Tested whether any single activity was worth 20+ points, so accounts with lots of small touches never qualified. Intent was not excluded at all, and the tier grouping was broken. Sums engagement points across the past 3 months, excludes all three intent types, and applies one clean US tier group.
MQA Carried a high/medium intent shortcut, and engagement points were counted per-activity rather than summed. Requires ≥40 summed points and a named engaged person. Intent no longer qualifies anything on its own.
i
If you see MQA "down" sharply, read it carefully The dashboard recomputes history against the current definition. Accounts that entered MQA under the old intent shortcut are no longer counted as having entered. It is a definitional restatement, not a drop in demand. Which specific accounts changed stages is still being verified.
08

FAQ

what it means, and the questions that follow
What it means
We’ve updated the US Journey so Engaged means at least 20 engagement points over three months, and MQA means at least 40 plus one engaged known person. Intent still helps us find and target interested accounts — it just no longer qualifies them as Engaged or MQA on its own.
The line that explains the whole change:
Researching a topic shows interest in the subject. Engaging with TP shows interest in us.
Does this mean intent is no longer working to push accounts into our ads?
No. Intent still targets and optimizes ads — keep adding relevant intent keywords to campaigns. We only changed how accounts qualify as Engaged or MQA: they now need engagement beyond intent signals.
So do we still need intent keywords on our campaigns?
Yes — it’s a required field and it should be. Intent helps optimize delivery alongside your account audience and people-targeting settings. Nothing about that changed.
By adding our intent keywords, are we still targeting those accounts? And why does engagement matter most?
Yes — your account list sets who we target, and intent keywords help Demandbase prioritize relevant people within those accounts. Engagement matters more for earning Engaged or MQA status because researching a topic shows interest in the subject; engaging with TP shows interest in us.
Why did the MQA number drop?
We removed the intent shortcut and changed how engagement points are counted. MQA now requires ≥40 cumulative non-intent engagement points plus ≥1 engaged known person. We’re still checking which accounts changed stages.
Can my campaign still move an account into Engaged or MQA?
Yes. Recorded campaign interactions can contribute engagement points under our scoring rules. Intent got them targeted; their recorded behavior is what can move them forward — a single click or visit doesn’t guarantee qualification on its own.
I can’t find an account in MQA. Where is it?
Check whether it’s already Opportunity or Customer. An account sits in one stage only — the highest it qualifies for.
Can I tell Sales which person was researching a topic?
No. Intent is account-level — it tells you the company is researching something, never who. Pair it with the named person’s actual behavior (page visits, opens, form fills), which you do have.
